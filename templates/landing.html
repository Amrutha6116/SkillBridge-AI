About The Platform

Built for Academic Excellence & Real-World Placement Drives

SkillBridge AI was engineered to address one of the largest challenges facing graduating engineers: knowing exactly what companies require and having a structured pathway to satisfy those expectations.

Our mathematical gap formulation guarantees transparency: Gap = Max(0, Role Benchmark − Measured Proficiency). This eliminates guesswork and provides students and placement coordinators with actionable academic intelligence.
